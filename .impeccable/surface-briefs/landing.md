## Direction contract

THESIS: A disciplined digital-studio poster, not a generic agency hero. Keep the reference's huge condensed filled/outlined lettering and pale tape, but remove the busy collage and give the moving logo and text separate bands.

OWN-WORLD: Approved MCode near-black, acid-green and off-white. Flat fields, fine rules, self-hosted Anton display and Barlow reading text, sharp rectangular controls. Preserve the logo files intact.

STORY: Visitors see MCode's creative-development direction, explore explicitly conceptual work, and prepare an enquiry without pretending it was submitted.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Thin masthead. The exact MCode symbol contours become a 48-unit-deep extruded mesh, projected in 3D and rotating around the vertical axis in a 12-second loop. A green halftone dot pass modulates by face lighting. The logo occupies its own stage above a straight white tape saying Design • Código • Conversão. The foreground reads FORA DA CAIXA. in filled/outlined Anton with a neon period. Work CTA and short description sit below, without overlaps. On mobile, the headline reflows to two lines, animation control moves under the logo, and artwork captions have reserved bands. Pause, reduced-motion, document visibility and offscreen suspension are implemented.

FORM: User-pinned reference, refined by explicit request for less clutter and a rotating extruded halftone MCode logo instead of anime. seed: none (user-pinned reference).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
